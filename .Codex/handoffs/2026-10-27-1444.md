## 8. Decisions, rationale, and invariants

- Delivery Phase 4 acceptance is separate from generated-project phase acceptance.
  Requirements, plan, permission and generated-phase approvals remain distinct.
- Review identity covers plan identity, phase attempt, staging commit, package
  attempt IDs, checks, artifact hashes and decisions. Same plan hash alone is
  insufficient. Canonical review payloads are immutable strings persisted by hash.
- Accepting promotes the reviewed commit through CAS on `refs/heads/accepted`.
  The management checkout stays at its existing tree with detached HEAD; source
  lives in retained worker/staging checkouts. Do not restore ordinary filtered
  checkout/reset operations to synchronize that management checkout.
- Acceptance enables a separate explicit successor start. Rejection needs fresh
  attempts. Old IDs, reviews, evidence and worktrees remain retained.
- Persist promotion/integration intent before external writes. Unknown outcomes
  stay blocked; never replay dispatch or promotion automatically. Promotion can
  be explicitly reconciled against saved review and actual ref. Uncertain
  integration still requires inspection and blocks repair.
- Startup reads saved Codex history before dispatch. Running/unknown turns stay
  blocked; cancelled turns may be inspected without being resumed. Corrections
  remain effective even if interruption fails.
- Preserve owned roots, raw byte inspection/materialization, sanitized Git policy,
  disabled hooks/filters/external diff, long-path support, output limits and deadlines.
- Trusted policy remains `git-inspection-only-v1`, supporting only the proposed
  argv `["git", "diff", "--check"]`. Generated tests/install scripts and arbitrary
  commands have no approved sandbox runner. Live qualification must use plans
  compatible with this policy, rather than weaken it to obtain a pass.
- Git checks establish scoped artifacts and whitespace, not application behavior
  or semantic compliance with textual exclusions. Worker claims are not tests.
- Keep loopback defaults, explicit execution enablement, managed ChatGPT login,
  profile/account binding and separate Codex tokens versus Nemotron dollars.
  No automatic sign-out, billing switch, account rotation or limit purchase.
- Provider calls for planning go through `LiveModels.call`; retain failure usage
  and session allowances. Do not create replacement sessions to bypass limits.
- Keep credentials, sessions, generated projects and qualification reports ignored
  under `.data/`. No generated backend/deployment/sync claim follows from source
